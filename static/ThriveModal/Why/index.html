THE 10/10 PLACEMENT PROCESS

How we find your hire

Most firms send you the best resume they have. We send you only candidates who meet every requirement you set, 10 out of 10. If someone is a 7, you never see them.

  1. 1
    We define your 10 with you
    A structured discovery call with someone who knows your industry. Skills, tools, schedule and experience, written down.
  2. 2
    We search four ways
    Passive sourcing, direct outreach, our candidate database and referrals, run by recruiters on the ground in the Philippines.
  3. 3
    We interview before you do
    Every candidate sits a structured video interview with us first. You only spend time on people who passed.
  4. 4
    You choose from at least three
    Each comes with a written summary and interview clips. We keep searching until every option is someone you could hire.
Shortlist in one to two weeks. Hire onboarded in three to five.

OFFSHORE STAFF AUGMENTATION

A dedicated full-time team member, without becoming the employer.

That is offshore staff augmentation: they work for you, we employ them. Here is how it differs from the two models you have probably already tried.

A recruiter
Fills the role, collects a fee and leaves. Onboarding, payroll and turnover are your problem.
An outsourcer
Gives you a share of a worker pool that also serves other clients. Nobody is on your team.
